Humming may be our oldest form of healing — an ancient technology of self-restoration through the sound of our own voice. Before instruments, before language, there was vibration. In the resonant chambers of caves, the human voice became both sound and sensation.

Singing Hollow is a sound amplifier you can step up to. Place your head into the opening, close your eyes and hum, and the carved cedar chamber returns your own frequency back to you.

Singing Hollow was designed together by Ben Hayward of Chinchilla Design and artist [Brent Comber](https://www.brentcomber.com), and installed at IDS West 2026. The sphere was modelled parametrically and CNC-milled in our North Vancouver shop. A skin of individually shaped western red cedar tiles, no two alike, wraps a yellow cedar core and funnels inward to a carved, softly lit hollow at its centre.
