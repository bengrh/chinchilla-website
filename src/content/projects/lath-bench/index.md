The Lath Bench is a sculptural seating piece developed with local artist and shop neighbour, Brent Comber. The form was modelled parametrically with each rib profile generated from a single ruled surface that shifts continuously along the length of the piece, producing a smooth organic undulation from end to end.

Construction uses yellow cedar laths, each cut to its precise profile, steam bent and fixed over a structural skeleton of CNC-cut ribs. This prototype example pictured here is affixed to a plywood frame but the production version is made to weather the elements and uses water resistant internal structural ribs.

This bench is a collaboration with [Brent Comber Originals](https://www.brentcomber.com) and is available for commission through his studio.
