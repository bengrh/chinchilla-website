Fay Mihailides Design came to us with a piece for [the client's space: what kind of room, what it is used for]. The brief was a long table with a presence of its own: a flat, quiet top, and an underside that steps down in a field of blocks, like a cave ceiling growing toward the floor. Internally we called it the Minecraft table. Stalactite Table is the name that stuck.

A stepped surface is simple to draw and awkward to build. Every block has to land on a true plane and meet its neighbours cleanly, or the stepping reads as sloppy rather than deliberate. We worked through the block size, the depth of the steps and the way the field thins toward the edges with Fay before anything was cut, so the final geometry came from one model and the shop could trust it.
